Before shipping commercially, read the terms on the Plus plan (output ownership and commercial use) and record the result here.

All assets are AI-generated illustrations or textures. None is a photo of a real place. No real person, logo or trademark is depicted (one candidate with Order-of-Christ crosses on sails was rejected). Label them in the UI: «Иллюстрация, создана ИИ». Post-processing (crop, resize, tile blending, normal/roughness derivation, alpha extraction, audio loop crossfade) was done with Python/ffmpeg scripts in /tmp/art.

## Use in the app (phase 1, 2026-10-10)

Shipped: `textures/roof_canudo_{256,512}.webp`, copied unchanged to `public/art/`. Loaded lazily after the scene is ready (src/art-pack.js), 256 px on M, 512 px on L and XL, skipped on P and S and with `?art=0`. It shades the roof tiles on the buildings (src/facades.js, luminance only, the roof keeps its own colour). A texture on geometry carries no label. Not wired: every other texture, the skies, the cloud sprites, the illustrations, the audio. `art/rejected/` was deleted after review (the loader was a flat blue mass, the barge picture carried Order of Christ crosses).
