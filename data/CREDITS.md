# Credits for the Douro bridge models (session 007)

The bridge builders in `src/models/porto/ponte-*.js` are drawn from published
dimensions (`data/dimensions.json` cites each number) and checked against
reference photographs. The photographs were looked at only; none is shipped
in the app. Free licences, from Wikimedia Commons:

Text sources for dimensions: pt.wikipedia (Ponte de D. Maria Pia, Ponte de
S. João, Ponte Infante D. Henrique, Ponte do Freixo, Ponte da Arrábida),
en.wikipedia (Dom Luís I Bridge, Arrábida Bridge, Freixo Bridge, Ponte de São
João), Wikidata, OpenStreetMap (ODbL) for the corridors. The shipped photos
of each bridge keep their credits in `data/landmarks.json` (`image_credit`).
